## New failures

### Common root cause — host pinned-memory exhaustion on the packed a3-800i nodes (18:08 batch)

Three of tonight's four failures are byte-identical in mechanism: at trainer init, at the **first** `sleep()` of the colocated vLLM engines (before training step 1; zero `step:` lines in all three logs), vllm-ascend's camem allocator tries to pin a host backup tensor for the weights offload and the **driver runs out of pinned host memory** (`r35636448782_nightlyCI_grpo_qwen3_5_2b_fsdp2_vllm_ascend.log`, same in both quick_start logs):

```
(vLLMHttpServer pid=29367) (Worker_TP1 pid=31098) File "/vllm-ascend/vllm_ascend/worker/worker.py", line 228, in sleep
    allocator.sleep(offload_tags=("weights",) if level == 1 else tuple())
  File "/vllm-ascend/vllm_ascend/device_allocator/camem.py", line 211, in sleep

```

verl frames are the documented init-time sleep path, unchanged on main:
`trainer_base.py:223 init → _setup:391 checkpoint_manager.sleep_replicas() → checkpoint_engine/base.py:469 → vllm_async_server.py:1425 → EngineCore sleep → camem.py:211` (quick_start megatron log; the fsdp2 log shows the same via `vllm_async_server`), ending in `Exception: Call to sleep method failed: Worker failed with error 'allocate_host_memory_slowpath...'`.

All three failing logs also carry a first-time **EI0020 port-collision diagnostic** on the same workers, e.g. `[PID: 31098] Communication_Error_Bind_IP_Port(EI0020): ... port 65536 have already been bound` — direct evidence of foreign processes on the same cards/node tonight (note: every NPU script exports `HCCL_NPU_SOCKET_PORT_RANGE=61000-61050`, so a bind at the default 65536 comes from a component/process outside that range). These allocations are tiny (0.6–2.2 B models, ~0.3–1 GB pinned shards); failing them means the node's pinned-host budget was already exhausted.

**Node packing tonight (via the Actions jobs API):** nvbsd (16-chip) ran fsdp2 (4 devices, 18:08:50–18:17:29, failed) **concurrently with** gspo-30b-megatron ([106454919695](https://github.com/verl-project/verl/actions/runs/35636448782/job/106454919695), success 18:09:16–18:45:03 — a 30B Megatron job with param+optimizer offload pinning O(100 s GB) host memory, its init overlapping fsdp2's failing pin at 18:17:06). tvqbd (8-chip) ran **four** quick_start jobs at once (each `n_gpus_per_node=8`, i.e. 4 processes per device); the two vLLM ones died at 18:14:30/18:14:38, the two sglang ones succeeded.
