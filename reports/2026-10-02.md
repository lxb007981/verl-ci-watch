This is the class that killed this same job on 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-07
(dedicated entry since pruned under the 14-day rule), and it is now tracked
upstream: open issue
[#6677](https://github.com/verl-project/verl/issues/6677) — "Multiple vLLM
instances on the same node may choose the same distributed init port and fail
with EADDRINUSE". The issue (with maintainer response) pins the mechanism in
vLLM itself: for single-node `MultiprocExecutor`, vLLM picks the
`init_process_group` port via `get_open_port()` (bind a throwaway socket,
release, bind later), two rollout engines starting on one node can pick the
same port in that gap, and **vLLM ignores externally provided ports on this
path, so verl cannot fix it**. Tonight's job runs multiple colocated vLLM
servers whose EngineCores were initializing concurrently (server pids
22355/22356/22357 all active at 17:25:29-33), matching that mechanism. verl
exonerated (#8010 off this path; job green 09-19..09-30 on this code).
Verdict KNOWN (#6677). Action: none in verl; retry is the present mitigation
(the race is rare). Yesterday's tee-log-loss signature did not recur
observably (training never started tonight).
