`NetworkManager::StartVnic/StartNic/StartHostNetAndListen`) the comm-init path returns it when HCCL cannot start one of
its listen sockets (host NIC, device NIC or vNIC): "already bound / no available port". Because the failure also occurs
with `auto`, a full TCP port range is unlikely to be the whole story — a device-side listen resource that is exhausted on
those nodes fits better, but that is a hypothesis. The log lines that name the NIC and port (`EI0019`/`EI0020`) go to the
HCCL plog on the node, not to the Actions log, so the trigger itself is not observable here. Caveat: the mirror may not
match the deployed CANN build (9.2.1 in this image).
