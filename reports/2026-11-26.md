sampling noise. There is nothing to fix in verl. The recipe repo publishes no a3 baseline for this job to point
`nightly_ascend.yml:102` at (its `baseline/a3/nightly_log` holds three other jobs' baselines), and no runner label
exists for the driver version. Upstream searches (`baseline_ppo_qwen3-8b`, `26.0.rc3`, `npu-smi driver`, the job
name) find no issue or PR about either the check failure or the driver versions.

1. Runner-admin: bring the `25.5.1` nodes to `26.0.rc3`, or label nodes by driver and schedule the perf-checked jobs on
   `26.0.rc3`. On those nodes the PPO job passes its perf check every night (5/5) and Qwen3-VL runs 15–17% under its
   baseline. It is also a 21–23% step-time saving on these two jobs. `26.0.rc3` is a release candidate, which suggests
   a staged rollout. Finishing it is the option that also turns these checks green; reverting would leave the fleet
   uniformly slow, with the PPO job failing its perf check every night as it did on all seven `25.5.1` nights.
2. Recipe-side (verl-ascend-recipe): widen or relativize the rewards threshold, and regenerate the memory baseline
   (22.23 GB recorded, 15.3–15.5 GB actual on all 12 checked nights).
